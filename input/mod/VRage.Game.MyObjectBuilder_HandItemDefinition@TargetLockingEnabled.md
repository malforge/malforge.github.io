## Summary

```csharp
public bool TargetLockingEnabled
```

Opts this hand weapon into target locking: focus rectangles while held, lock-on while aiming down sights, and the lead indicator. Whether a fired missile then homes on the locked target is the ammo's decision.

## Returns

[bool](https://docs.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/api/System.Boolean?view=netframework-4.6)
